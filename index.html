Why Northgate Realty

Northgate Realty is a concept agency made for this portfolio demo, so these are the principles the site is designed around.

  • Clear information

    Listings state what is known and what is not, with no hidden extras.

  • A simple enquiry

    One short form, with only a name and an email required.

  • Respect for your details

    Your information is used only to reply to your enquiry.
